Parallelism spectrum — disagreement is real

Số lượng agent chạy đồng thời. Không có industry consensus. Plan-mode + verification — consensus.

5 pattern recurring across all 8 luminary

01
Plan mode is consensusCherny, Ronacher, Hashimoto, Karpathy đều start với planning trước generation. Cherny: "never a line of code until plan approved" — single most repeated practice.
02
Verification loop > raw speedCherny "2-3× quality from feedback loop", Yang "review every line", Hashimoto "QA while agent codes", Yegge "plan-implement-review-test loop". Test, type checker, browser MCP — anything machine-checkable là moat.
03
Parallelism real but disputedCherny (10-15), Yegge (20-30), Hashimoto (always-on bg), Litt (kanban-managed) lean heavy. Yang + Litt push back: comprehension và surgical focus matter more. Không có consensus.
04
Skills + CLAUDE.md beat MCPWillison + Ronacher cùng public downgrade MCP. Skills (post-Oct 2025) và well-tuned CLAUDE.md (Karpathy, Cherny team file) là cái people thực sự dùng day-to-day. SEONGON: invest first vào CLAUDE.md + skills, treat MCP optional.
05
Senior nhất là người opinionated nhất về limitsKarpathy nanochat retreat, Yang "read every line", Litt surgeon framing — endorsement mạnh nhất luôn kèm explicit limits. More credible signal than uniform hype: những người này đã nghĩ về where it breaks.

6 pattern recurring across non-engineer leader

01
Personal-infrastructure glueMullenweg's Sonos/calendar/PDF utilities, Lenny's storage cleanup + Sheet randomizer, Newton's writing-archive search. Common thread: "tools too small to justify hiring engineer for, but too annoying to do manually." Claude Code wins over ChatGPT vì act on local files.
02
Personal websites + brand surfaceNewton replaced Squarespace (saved $200/yr). Levels rebuilt pieter.com như 3D Three.js. Non-engineer leader đang kill SaaS subscription bằng weekend Claude Code project.
03
Strategy/coaching surface, không phải codeLavingia 9 slash command. Tan gstack roles. Claude Code dùng như structured conversation partner — value là persistent skill definition + filesystem context.
04
Run-it-headless infrastructureLevels VPS pattern, Srinivas Perplexity Computer orchestration. Leaders treat Claude Code như background worker on server. Biggest divergence từ ChatGPT-style use.
05
"Tools, not text"Casey Newton framing — "LLMs' power to generate tools" — recurs implicitly trong Mullenweg, Tan, Lavingia. Non-engineer dùng Claude Code để manufacture small bespoke applications, không phải draft documents.
06
Skill pack as portable artifactTan + Lavingia ship public skill repo. "Publish your CLAUDE.md / skills" trở thành form of thought leadership distinct từ blog or podcast.
