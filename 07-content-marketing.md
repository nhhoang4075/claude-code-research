# Content Marketing — Claude Code Adoption Evidence

**SEONGON relevance:** Indirect. SEONGON sells SEO and ads, not content marketing as a standalone service — but content production sits inside their SEO retainers, and the patterns here transfer to that work.

## The Animalz pattern (most-cited content-marketing reference)

Animalz published the canonical "Claude Code for Content Marketers" guide. Six named slash-commands:

| Skill | Function |
|---|---|
| `/write` | 8-phase article creation: Foundation → Thesis → Structure → Research → Outline → Introduction → Drafting → Review |
| `/copywrite` | 7-phase conversion copy with quality testing against three rules |
| `/design-reference` | Iterative visual direction research and prototyping |
| `/image-prompt` | Production-quality prompts for AI image generation; builds reusable brand library |
| `/generate-style-guide` | Parallel analysis of 8 editorial dimensions → comprehensive brand standards |
| `/style-check` | Validates new content against established guidelines |

Plus **content audits** (analyzing blog exports for structural issues + decay), **audience research** (aggregating questions from forums + communities), and **competitive analysis** (reverse-engineering competitor messaging).

Reported time savings: "minutes" for blog analysis vs. "hours of spreadsheet work"; same-day tool development.

## Anti-AI-slop production layer (superseo-skills)

The most operationally useful piece of content-marketing IP for an SEO agency:

- **Tiered banned vocabulary** — words that flag content as AI-written
- **Structural pattern detection** — identifies "the AI shape" of paragraphs
- **The Horoscope Test** — a specific quality benchmark
- **Voice injection** — embeds specific writing-voice characteristics
- **Search intent matching** — ensures content matches the SERP intent class
- **E-E-A-T signal embedding** — authentic expertise signals throughout

Production-tested at the InhouseSEO agency. Ports directly to a content-production agency context; would need Vietnamese-language adaptation.

## Operating-model implications for SEONGON

### Content sitting inside SEO retainers

The opportunity isn't to launch a new content marketing service line — it's to **reduce fulfillment cost on the content-production component of existing SEO retainers**. Specifically:

- Use the superseo-skills anti-slop ruleset on every piece of generated content
- Use Animalz `/write` 8-phase skill as the production template
- Use `/style-check` per-client based on a Brand Skill (see 06-brand-pr.md)

This compounds with the SEO time-savings already documented (20–30 monthly hours per account → 6–8 hours).

### Content-quality risks to manage

1. **AI-detection risk**: Vietnamese-language AI-content detectors are catching up to English ones. The anti-slop layer is *necessary*, not optional.
2. **Brand-voice risk**: Each client has a distinct voice. Don't share style-guides across clients.
3. **Citation hygiene**: Claude can hallucinate sources. Every published claim with a citation needs human verification.

## Suggested SEONGON pilot scope (content slice)

**Pilot team**: 1 senior content editor + 2 content writers (one English-fluent for cross-reference, both Vietnamese-native).

**Pilot duration**: 4 weeks.

**Targets**:
- Adapt superseo-skills anti-slop ruleset to Vietnamese (week 1–2)
- Run Animalz `/write` 8-phase skill on 5 SEO retainer pieces (week 3)
- A/B compare time-to-publish + post-publish ranking outcomes against current process (week 4)

**Decision gate**:
- If anti-slop adaptation passes Vietnamese AI-detector tests, deploy across all SEO retainer content.
- If `/write` reduces editor pass time substantially without quality regression, formalize as the SEO content production SOP.
